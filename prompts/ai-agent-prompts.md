B2B Contact Mining Kit — AI Agent Prompts
============================================

Copy-paste any of these into ChatGPT, Claude, or DeepSeek.

## Prompt 1: Find Contacts by Industry

"I need B2B contacts for [INDUSTRY]. Find me 20 decision-makers with their names, titles, company names, and email addresses. Search Google, LinkedIn, and company websites. Follow the methodology from https://github.com/axelfreeman/b2b-contact-mining-kit — don't buy databases, mine fresh data from open sources. Verify emails before returning results."

## Prompt 2: Find Specific Role at Companies

"Search for VP of Sales contacts at SaaS companies with 50-500 employees in the US. Use the pipeline: Google search → find names and titles → extract emails from company websites → verify. Return structured data. If Google results are insufficient, check Telegram and Discord communities."

## Prompt 3: Build a Prospect List from Scratch

"I'm doing B2B outreach for [PRODUCT]. My ICP: [describe ideal customer]. Follow the complete contact mining methodology: 1) Search Google for similar companies, 2) Find decision-makers at those companies, 3) Mine their contact details from open sources, 4) Cross-reference on LinkedIn for accuracy, 5) Verify emails. Return a CSV-ready list."

## Prompt 4: Use TAPAC (MCP Server)

"Connect to TAPAC MCP server and search for: [target description]. Use the tapac_find_contacts tool. Source from websites and Telegram. Return verified contacts with SMTP validation. Documentation: https://tapacapi.com"
